This is part 5 of #957: the TP3 adaptive-verification constants.

Rationale:
5. On TP=3 each rank executes a wider MoE intermediate (768 vs 576) and 24 MLA heads (2 head tiles vs 1), increasing marginal verification row cost above the 16-row MMA knee. Use topology-aware HIGH_ROW_COST_SCALE_TP3 = 0.85 and MIN_CALIBRATED_DEAD_ROWS_TAU_TP3 = 0.12 when tp_size == 3.

Quality implications:
- Only which draft rows a TP=3 step verifies changes. Every admitted row is verified by the same rejection sampler, so temperature-0 text must not change; sampled realizations may change.
- TP sizes other than 3 select the existing constants (multi-request scale 0.55, no floor) and keep every budget.
