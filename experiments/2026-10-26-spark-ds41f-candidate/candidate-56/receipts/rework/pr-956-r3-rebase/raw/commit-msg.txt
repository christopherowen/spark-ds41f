perf(dsv41): SP ratio-2 compressor projection and CED encoder-only chunks

Upstream base: candidate-54 vLLM patch series (patch head 4e8133ff10a125b90753c2727f162e29fe37f30c, tree 5f021d9a7f01c03e2389dd3d8021759cfa19ea34) plus #957 part 1 (the replicated BF16 compressor through TP4; tree 3959b21147bc9f98f4ebcd48e2c80e0f42d4e2ef).

Rationale:
1. On encoder layers 2, 8, and 14 (compress_ratio == 2), DeepseekCompressor._sp_enabled was restricted to compress_ratio == 1. During SP prefill, these three layers disabled _gather_front, gathered the full [rows, 5120] BF16 hidden state across TP ranks before attention, ran fused_wqa_wkv and fused_wkv_wgate on all rows on every rank, and performed an extra column all-gather for the compressor projection. Enable _sp_enabled for compress_ratio in (1, 2) using bf16_gemm_two(512, 5120, out_dtype="float32", shards=split["shards"], blocked=split["blocked"]) on sp_rows.local_rows and sp_rows.gather_many(local_values, local_gates), and enable _gather_front on layers 2, 8, and 14 so both the attention front and compressor project only local SP rows.
2. On multi-chunk prefill requests, non-final chunks ending at or before the CED decoder replay floor (start + q <= floor) select 0 real decoder replay rows (padded to 1 dummy row [0] solely to keep compact decoder tensors non-empty). Previously, the forward pass still executed layer 20's decoder attention and MoE plus all 19 decoder layers (layers 21..39) on that dummy row and ran two [rows, 5120] scatter_rows zero-fills. Track encoder_only on CEDState when every request in the step is a non-final prefill chunk ending at or before floor, stop at the CED boundary layer immediately after mhc.pre and prepare_global_kv, and return preallocated zeros.

Quality implications:
Lossless and bit-exact. bf16_gemm_two with shards=5, blocked=True reproduces the column-parallel fused_wkv_wgate split-K reduction bit for bit, and non-final prefill chunks ending at or before the CED replay floor have no selected decoder replay rows or sampled output logits.
