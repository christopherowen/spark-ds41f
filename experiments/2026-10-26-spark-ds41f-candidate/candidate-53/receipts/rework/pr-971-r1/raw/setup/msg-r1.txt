perf(spec_decode): use cumulative survival in copy_apply and cap single-req copy tile spill

Base: 1107 vLLM patch head 320021e3c4dbb202d911ab70e2cd924c75420488 (tree ed1bdb7a7684da33d403a055521ac0c4dd7ca670).

Rationale:
1. copy_apply_kernel previously gated CopyDrafter replacement against the sum of raw conditional DSpark probabilities (sum_{j=0}^{block-1} dspark_confidences[r, j]) rather than cumulative survival yield (sum_{j=0}^{block-1} prod_{i=0}^j dspark_confidences[r, i]). Because a 5-draft DSpark block with conditional confidences [0.70, 0.65, 0.60, 0.55, 0.50] has raw sum 3.00 but cumulative survival 1.6532, short 2- and 3-token copy continuations (expected yield 1.90 and 2.85 at 0.95 confidence) were rejected in favor of lower-yield DSpark proposals. Accumulating dspark_survival *= dspark_confidences[r, j] aligns copy_apply with AdaptiveVerificationManager.get_num_tokens.
2. AdaptiveVerificationManager._step_cost applied the high-concurrency coalescing discount (HIGH_ROW_COST_SCALE = 0.55 above HIGH_ROW_COST_KNEE = 16 rows) even when num_sampling_requests == 1. At row_cost = 0.035, a single-request 16-draft copy proposal at the 0.95 initial prior crossed 16 verification rows into 17 rows (24-row CUDA graph, 2 Tensor Core MMA waves of 16 rows). Applying SINGLE_REQ_TILE_SPILL_SCALE = 2.0 above 16 rows when num_sampling_requests <= 1 (and setting scheduled_drafts[0] = draft_budget directly in reallocate_drafts when num_reqs == 1) caps initial 0.95-prior single-request copy proposals at 15 drafts (16 rows = 1 MMA tile in the 16-row CUDA graph), while admitting all 16 drafts once online copy accuracy reaches >= 0.97 or when multiple requests share the batch (c2+).

Quality:
Bit-identical target and drafter arithmetic; temperature-0 greedy verification remains invariant to draft source and to the draft budget.
