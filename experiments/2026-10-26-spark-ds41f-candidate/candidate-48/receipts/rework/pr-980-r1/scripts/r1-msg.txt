perf(spec_decode): elide TP confidence broadcasts and lockstep TP sampling seeds

Upstream-Base: https://github.com/local-inference-lab/vllm.git@19f2c20ed4d64ae22bd009ceacadd85e2a81e354
Upstream-Status: local

Rationale:
1. SamplingStates.add_request previously drew unseeded per-request seeds from
   the process-global NumPy RNG (np.random.randint). Any rank-asymmetric call
   to np.random desynchronized seeds across TP ranks, causing gumbel_sample
   and rejection_sample to draw different draft and verified tokens per rank,
   diverging DSparkConfidenceHead inputs and OnlineAcceptanceEstimator IRLS
   parameters across TP ranks when the per-step confidence broadcast is elided.
   Give SamplingStates an isolated deterministically seeded generator
   (self._seed_rng = np.random.default_rng(0)) so all TP ranks assign
   bit-identical seeds to unseeded requests in lockstep.
2. Sampler.sample previously dispatched unseeded top_k/top_p requests to
   flashinfer_sample, which ignores per-request seeds and uses rank-local CUDA
   RNG state. When TP world_size > 1, route top_k/top_p sampling through the
   deterministic counter-based TileLang top-k/top-p + gumbel_sample path keyed
   by (seeds.gpu, pos).
3. Mark OnlineAcceptanceEstimator as tp_invariant = True, reuse persistent
   _scratch_stats buffers in predict(), expose predict_from_stats() for
   vocab-parallel draft stats, and expose DraftModelSpeculator.tp_invariant_confidence.
   The model runner hands the speculator's value to AdaptiveVerificationManager
   when it creates it, so logit- and shard-stat-based acceptance estimation also
   elides the per-step rank-0 confidence broadcast on rank-ordered TP
   communicators. AdaptiveVerificationManager.record_confidences is unchanged:
   it reads that stored flag as before, so the greedy DSpark decode step (whose
   confidence head already elides the broadcast) runs no line of this patch.

Quality:
Lossless. Preserves exact sampling distributions while guaranteeing 100%
cross-rank bitwise determinism across TP ranks for both greedy and sampled
speculative decoding.
