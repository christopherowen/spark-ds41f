## Harness (scripts/, ours; mounted read-only at /h)
# rw.sh: one container per run: spark-ds41f:1107 with an arm's 26 files bind-mounted read-only over
#   /opt/spark-ds41f/candidate/vllm, --entrypoint python3, the served checkpoint's HF hub dir at /hub (ro; only
#   config.json is read), serving env (VLLM_USE_V2_MODEL_RUNNER=1, VLLM_L2_PREFETCH_KERNELS=tilelang,
#   VLLM_L2_PREFETCH_GRID=2, VLLM_USE_BREAKABLE_CUDAGRAPH=0, VLLM_DS41_HEAD_DTYPE=float32,
#   CUDA_DEVICE_MAX_CONNECTIONS=32), one TileLang cache dir per arm.
# ffnlayer.py: builds vLLM's own DeepseekV4DecoderLayer (deepseek_v4_1/nvidia/model.py) under a VllmConfig from
#   EngineArgs (the checkpoint's config.json; tilelang attention/linear/MoE backends; max_num_batched_tokens 16384;
#   TP 1 with moe_intermediate_size 576 / 768 = TP4 / TP3 per-rank shapes), attention replaced by a stub that owns
#   nothing; random weights in checkpoint dtypes through process_weights_after_loading and
#   prepare.declare_modules; the FFN sublayer is forward()'s second half (mhc.post_pre, then ffn(x, input_ids)
#   inside kernels.mxfp8_inputs) under a forward context with the V2 runner's is_padding. The MoE runs through
#   MoERunner._forward_impl / _apply_quant_method, SharedExperts, TileLangGateRouter and TileLangExperts'
#   MoeExpertsV41Plan, captured in torch.cuda.CUDAGraph.
# ffnbench.py (session 1): per shape 6 layers; per M two graphs: chain (6 layers back to back, each: issue the
#   layer's FFN L2 prefetch, FFN sublayer, join) timed around 10 replays; cold (each layer: write 96 MiB, then the
#   same between two in-graph timing events). >= 1.5 s of replays before the first window; 15 windows
#   interleaving every (mode, M); medians. Eager pass traces the shared-expert schedule; every graph's outputs
#   hashed (eager, after capture, after timing).
# pairbench.py (session 2): the same layers in one process with candidate-47's moe.py, moe_runner.py and
#   shared_experts.py at /alt; their own functions (compiled from source by ast in the live modules' namespace)
#   are switched in while a variant's graphs are captured (kernel: bound as the plan's decode swiglu kernel;
#   hook: MoERunner._forward_impl/_apply_quant_method, SharedExperts.maybe_forward_async/record_input_ready).
#   12 windows; in each, every variant's graph of a (mode, M) in shuffled order; paired deltas per window.
# summarize.py / pairsum.py: the tables in result.json.
