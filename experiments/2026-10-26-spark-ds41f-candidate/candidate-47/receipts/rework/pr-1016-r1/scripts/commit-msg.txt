perf(dsv41): align 64-row Q-B decode tiles with 128x128 tile-contiguous weights

Base: 1107 vLLM patch head 2756478b60b90700b9918b457aeb38657da637b5. Replaces
candidate-47's 0768 (PR #1016 as submitted). It touches only gemm.py and its
test, which no other patch of the 0762..0769 series changes.

Rationale:
Q-B (wq_b) at TP4/TP5 (8192 x 1280) and TP3 (12288 x 1280) keeps its FP8 weight
in 128x128 tile-contiguous slabs (pack_weight_tiles, 16 KiB per tile). 1107's
64-row decode tiles for both shapes use block_N = 64, so two CTAs share each
slab, each reading an 8 KiB half per K step. The grid doubles to 128 / 192 CTAs
on 48 SMs, and every CTA repeats the A and SFA loads. With block_N = 128,
block_K = 128 and num_stages = 2 (1107's 12288 tile already uses 2 stages), each
CTA reads whole slabs, and the 64 / 96 CTAs still cover the 48 SMs.

Only the 64-row Q-B tiles change. The submitted revision also widened the
32-row Q-B tiles and the 16-row (4096, 1280) tile, dropped threads=64 from the
64-row (4096, 1280) tile, and registered (16384|6144|2048, 1280). This revision
keeps 1107's settings for all of those:
- In the model, the (4096, 1280) decode tiles serve only TP8's Q-B. The indexer
  Q-B runs decode_rope_mxfp4, which linear.py builds with the kernel defaults
  (block_M 16, block_N 128, 2 stages, 256 threads). It never reads
  DECODE_FP8_CONFIGS. On the TP8 Q-B, dropping threads=64 does not speed up the
  64-row tile (M=48: +0.2 us warm, +1.1 us cold, mean of five processes). The
  16-row block_N = 128 tile is faster at M <= 6 but slower at M = 12 (+0.4 us
  warm in every process).
- The chained bench builds its indexer kernel from the (4096, 1280) tiles. On
  that kernel, block_N = 128 at 128 threads halves the rows of each serial
  RoPE/MXFP4 epilogue pass (2 * threads / block_N rows). M = 12 therefore needs
  six dependent passes instead of three, which is the bench's M = 12 slowdown.
- The 32-row tiles gained nothing at M = 24 beyond the interval. Single
  processes put the TP3 pair 4..6 us slower with the weights in L2.
- The TP2/TP6/TP7 shapes are outside the chained Q-B bench. They need their own
  change and their own measurement: TP6/TP7's trimmed rank unpacks and repacks
  Q-B.

Quality:
block_N and num_stages only change which CTA computes which output columns and
how the loads are pipelined. Each output accumulates K in the same order, so
decode outputs are bit-identical (torch.equal) to 1107's tiles, to row-major
decode and to prefill.
