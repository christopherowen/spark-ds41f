perf(dsv41): fuse SP prefill local attention front and keep index_weights local

Base: 1107 vLLM patch head 2756478b60b90700b9918b457aeb38657da637b5.

Rationale:
In DeepSeek-V4.1 sequence-parallel prefill (`sp_rows is not None`) across all 54 layers without a KV compressor (`self.compressor is None` and `replicated_sp_prefill == True`), `_gather_front` previously ran `q_norm` (`2,560 B/row` BF16), `kv_norm` (`1,024 B/row` BF16), and `indexer.weights_proj` (`64 B/row` BF16) on local `L` rows, packed them via `copy_columns`, all-gathered `3,648 B/row` of BF16 across all TP ranks, unpacked via `split_columns`, and then ran `cast_mxfp8(qr)` and `_cache_context_kv(kv, positions)` (`rope_` + `write_cache(..., cache_kind="swa")`) redundantly on all `T` gathered rows on every rank. However:
1. Neither `self.wq_b` nor `self.indexer.wq_b` reads the BF16 `qr` values when `mxfp8_inputs` (`q_cast`) is supplied.
2. `forward_mqa` never reads its `kv` argument once the SWA cache pages are written.
3. In a step with prefill rows, `_forward` splits the indexer by rank: `_index_query` slices `index_weights[first:last]` where `[first, last) = [split.start, split.start + split.real_rows)`, so each rank only consumes its own local `real_rows` slice unless `_INDEX_SPLIT_CHECK` is enabled. A decode step (`swa.is_decode`) is not split: every rank scores all `T` rows.

Introduce:
- `sp_attention_front_kernel` / `sp_attention_front` in `vllm/models/deepseek_v4_1/tilelang/front.py`: a single fused TileLang kernel on local `real_rows` (`qr_kv[:real, :1792]`) that RMS-normalizes `qr` (`1280`) and casts it directly to MXFP8 (`1,280 B` E4M3 values + `40 B` UE8M0 scales = `1,320 B/row`), and RMS-normalizes `kv` (`512`), applies NeoX RoPE on the 64 dim-pairs using `positions[start:start + real]`, and packs the 528-byte SWA KV record (`512 B` E4M3 + `16 B` UE8M0 scales) per row.
- `swa_records_kernel` / `write_swa_records` in `vllm/models/deepseek_v4_1/tilelang/cache_writer.py`: scatters gathered 528-byte SWA records (`[T, 528]`) into paged `swa_cache_layer.kv_cache` at `slot_mapping[:T]` via 128-bit `dsv41_copy_b128` vector copies.
- `_sp_front` in `vllm/models/deepseek_v4_1/attention.py`: replaces `_gather_front`, keeping `index_weights = self.indexer.weights_proj(hidden_states)[:real]` rank-local where `_forward` splits the indexer (a step with prefill rows), gathering them to all `T` rows in a decode step and under `_INDEX_SPLIT_CHECK`, and all-gathering `(local_q_cast, local_records)` via `sp_rows.gather_mxfp8` (`1,848 B/row` vs `3,648 B/row`, `-49.34%` collective payload; `-1,518.75 MiB` across 54 layers at `T=16,384`, `TP=4`), then scattering SWA records into `swa_cache_layer.kv_cache`.

Round 1 (candidate-50):
The first version kept `index_weights` rank-local in every SP forward. A decode step can run sequence-parallel: the warm-up runs each eager token count past the capture limit once (`warm_tail_shapes`), and at tp4-ring the first, `T=214` (16 requests of 13-14 rows, within the copy-draft decode threshold of 17 rows), is past the SP threshold of 205 rows. `_forward` does not split a decode step, so `_index_query(..., 0, T)` took `index_weights[0:214]` of a rank's 54 local rows while the index queries had 214 rows, and the boot failed in the scorer (`indexer_score input Weights shape[0] violates packed ABI constraint; expected: 64, got: 54`). `_sp_front` now reads the step's SWA metadata first and keeps the head weights local only where `_forward` splits the indexer; otherwise it gathers them (`sp_rows.gather`, `64 B/row`, as `_INDEX_SPLIT_CHECK` already did). A step with prefill rows launches the same kernels and collectives as before. Besides the warm-up, a serving step whose requests are all short (each within the decode threshold, together past the SP threshold) is such a decode step too.

Quality:
100% bit-exact (`torch.equal`, 0 uint16/uint8 diffs) against the 5-kernel reference sequence (`q_norm`, `cast_mxfp8`, `kv_norm`, `rope_`, `write_cache`) across `TP=3` and `TP=4` including non-divisible token counts (`real_rows < local_rows` on tail ranks).
Round 1: vLLM's own TileLang attention of layers 20 (KV, index and candidate source) and 24 (index source without a compressor) at TP3, TP4 and TP8 rank shapes on one GPU, the SP all-gather simulated across the ranks: layer 24's attention output (WO's input), top-k indices and SWA cache pages on every rank are byte-identical to the tree without this patch in decode steps (the `T=214` warm-up step, and `T=216` with context) and prefill steps (`T=4096`, `T=4097`, and 90 decode rows beside a 4006-row prefill), on two boots. The first version fails both decode steps at TP3, TP4 and TP8 as the tp4-ring boot did.
